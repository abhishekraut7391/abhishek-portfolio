# Om Arts Business Website

**Project type:** Practical business website concept  
**Author:** Abhishek Balkrishna Raut

## Overview

A website concept for presenting Om Arts services, including custom name plates, rubber stamps, UV printing, laser engraving, signage, and related shop services.

## Intended purpose

- Present business services clearly.
- Help visitors discover relevant products and customisation options.
- Provide a simple way to contact the business.

## Technology stack

The portfolio describes the website as a front-end business site using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap.
